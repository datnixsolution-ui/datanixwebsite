Precision Right-to-Left (RTL) Arabic Processing

Processing Arabic datasets requires specialized expertise in Right-to-Left typography, complex cursive ligature rendering, diacritic preservation, and Unicode UTF-8 bidirectional algorithms. Standard software frequently reverses digits, fragments joined characters, and misaligns table columns.

DATNIX SOLUTIONS maintains dedicated Arabic Language Processing Pods. Staffed with trained Arabic transcription specialists and supervised by bilingual quality leads, we deliver flawless data conversion for clients in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, and global publishing sectors.

Specialized Capabilities:

  • ✓
    Advanced Arabic OCR & Proofreading: Conversion of scanned books, journals, and non-searchable Arabic PDFs into editable Microsoft Word, Excel, and searchable PDF/A with perfect font mapping.
  • ✓
    Legal Deed & Government Archive Transcription: Manual keying and multi-tier verification of handwritten and vintage calligraphic deeds, court registers, and municipal land records.
  • ✓
    Bilingual Arabic-English Cataloging: Parallel data keying for e-commerce, pharmaceutical, and retail inventories with accurate transliteration and category taxonomy mapping.
  • ✓
    Arabic ePUB & XML Structuring: Creation of validated Arabic ePUB 3.0 e-books with proper RTL spine orientation, CSS stylesheets, and embedded Arabic OpenType typography.
