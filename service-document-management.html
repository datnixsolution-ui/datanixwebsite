Transforming Paper Chaos into Searchable Digital Intelligence

Organizations generate massive volumes of physical paperwork, scanned contracts, regulatory forms, and unorganized PDF files. Without structured indexing and standardized naming conventions, finding critical documents during audits, litigation, or client inquiries becomes frustrating and slow.

DATNIX SOLUTIONS provides comprehensive Document Management services that bridge physical documents and digital repositories. We handle high-volume digitisation, format conversions (Word, PDF, TIFF, Excel), human-verified OCR text extraction, and metadata tagging.

Our team organizes your files into logical, hierarchical folder structures with uniform naming conventions, ensuring that every record can be retrieved in seconds while meeting your compliance and data governance standards.

Scope of Work

Key Document Management Capabilities

We organize, digitize, and classify enterprise records into compliant, audit-ready digital repositories.

Capability 01

Archive Digitisation

Systematic conversion of physical file rooms, paper forms, historical ledgers, and folders into high-resolution, lightweight digital assets.

Capability 02

Document Conversion

Converting between Word, PDF, TIFF, JPEG, Excel, and XML formats with preservation of layout, fonts, headers, footers, and table columns.

Capability 03

Metadata Tagging & Indexing

Tagging every file with standardized index parameters including document type, counterparty name, effective dates, contract IDs, and serial numbers.

Capability 04

Categorisation & Classification

Sorting mixed document batches into accurate functional categories (e.g. Invoices, NDAs, Inspection Reports, Board Resolutions, Human Resources).

Capability 05

File Organisation & Hierarchy

Restructuring digital file shares, Google Drive, SharePoint, and Dropbox with uniform naming standards (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD_Category_ID.pdf`).

Capability 06

OCR Text Verification

Auditing OCR scan outputs against original scans to correct misread characters, smudged numbers, and garbled table cells before release.
