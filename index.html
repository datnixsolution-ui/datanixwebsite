How does DATNIX SOLUTIONS handle specialized Arabic data conversion and RTL OCR?
Linguistic Technology

Unlike generic OCR software that struggles with right-to-left scripts, our dedicated Arabic Digitization Hub utilizes specialized neural OCR models combined with native Arabic linguists. We preserve full diacritical marks (Tashkeel), format bidirectional (BiDi) mixed English-Arabic text seamlessly, and transcribe historical Islamic manuscripts and GCC legal deeds with 99.9% accuracy.

What digital publishing outputs are supported in OCR and XML conversion?
Publishing Architecture

We convert legacy printed material, PDFs, and microfilms into modern, accessible publishing formats:

  • XML: JATS XML for scholarly and medical journals, TEI XML for academic libraries, and custom schema-validated XML with DTD validation.
  • ePUB: Reflowable and fixed-layout ePUB 3.0 optimized for Apple Books, Amazon Kindle, Kobo, and Google Play.
  • HTML5 & MathML: Accessible semantic web layouts with interactive equations and structured tables.
  • Searchable Archival PDF: High-compression PDF/A with hidden OCR text layers for long-term document retention.
