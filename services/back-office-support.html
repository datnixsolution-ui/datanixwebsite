Scope of Work

Key Back Office Capabilities

We handle essential administrative workflows to keep your internal teams focused on core strategy.

Operation 01

Administrative Operations

Handling daily operational execution including registration checks, application processing, customer correspondence sorting, and transaction filing.

Operation 02

Record Maintenance

Continuous updating of master customer profiles, vendor directories, account statuses, subscription records, and contact histories across your business systems.

Operation 03

Routine Business Support

Systematic daily operational assistance including shift log upkeep, spreadsheet updates, milestone notifications, and exception logging for manager review.

Operation 04

Order & Invoice Verification

Cross-checking customer purchase orders against vendor delivery receipts, freight tariffs, and billing statements to prevent invoicing discrepancies.

Operation 05

Application & Intake Vetting

Reviewing submitted documents for completeness, verifying identity proofs, validating mandatory fields, and routing flagged records to compliance teams.

Operation 06

Daily Reporting & Handover

Providing transparent shift summaries, completed volume metrics, pending items, and SLA adherence numbers directly to your department head.
