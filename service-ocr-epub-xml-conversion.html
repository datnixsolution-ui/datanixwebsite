Enterprise OCR & Digital Publishing Workflows

Organizations sitting on vast paper archives, research libraries, legal cases, and historical records face significant challenges making this content accessible, searchable, and monetizable. Simple automated desktop OCR frequently generates character misrecognitions, loses complex multi-column typography, drops mathematical equations, and damages tabular formatting.

DATNIX SOLUTIONS provides a comprehensive Human-in-the-Loop OCR & Conversion Pipeline. We combine commercial high-speed OCR engines (Abbyy FineReader Corporate, OmniPage) with multi-stage human proofreading and automated schema validation.

Core Deliverables & Output Formats:

  • ✓
    ePUB 3.0 & MOBI / KF8 Formatting: Reflowable e-books and fixed-layout digital publications with interactive table of contents, embedded fonts, and cross-device testing (Apple Books, Kindle, Kobo).
  • ✓
    Valid Structured XML Conversion: Strict adherence to international DTD and XSD schemas including JATS (Journal Article Tag Suite), TEI (Text Encoding Initiative), and DocBook XML.
  • ✓
    Scientific & Mathematical Typesetting: High-accuracy capture of chemical structures, LaTeX equations, and MathML encoding without missing symbols.
  • ✓
    Complex Table & Financial Ledger Extraction: Conversion of sprawling multi-page balance sheets, government registers, and census data into clean Excel, CSV, or structured database tables.
