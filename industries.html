1. E-Commerce & Retail

SKU Management Catalog Maintenance Multi-Channel Feeds

Transcribing supplier specification sheets, uploading bulk product listings, entering variant options (size, color, weight), and populating pricing schedules.

Reconciling order transaction logs, cross-referencing return slips against inventory adjustments, and tracking vendor intake queues for on-time launches.
