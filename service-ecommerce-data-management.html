Scope of Work

Key E-Commerce Data Capabilities

We handle everything from initial SKU onboarding to seasonal updates and attribute hygiene.

Capability 01

New Product Listing

Compiling and uploading bulk product titles, bullet features, long-form descriptions, standard barcodes (UPC/EAN), and brand attributions.

Capability 02

Catalogue Updates & Pricing

Executing scheduled price adjustments, promotional discounts, clearance tags, and supplier price revisions across thousands of SKUs accurately.

Capability 03

Product Information Management (PIM)

Maintaining clean master product data, technical specification tables, material composition tags, and regulatory hazard classifications.

Capability 04

Product Variant Mapping

Configuring parent-child SKU matrices for multi-dimensional options such as apparel sizes, material finishes, and packaging bundles.

Capability 05

Image-to-SKU Matching

Associating manufacturer photography, gallery angles, swatch thumbnails, and technical diagrams with the exact corresponding product SKU.

Capability 06

Category Taxonomy Standardization

Aligning product categories with marketplace navigation trees and search filters to optimize customer discovery and conversion rates.
