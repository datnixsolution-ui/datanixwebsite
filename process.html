The 5 Core Stages of Execution

Each stage incorporates defined responsibilities, documented checklists, and quality gates to maintain complete operational consistency.

01
Stage One

Requirement Understanding

Understand the client's scope, instructions and expected output. We conduct a structured onboarding review to evaluate source materials, target formats, data schemas, and turnaround expectations before any processing begins.

Key Operational Activities:
  • Detailed intake review of source files (scans, PDFs, portal records, spreadsheets)
  • Definition of required data fields, syntax rules, and normalization guidelines
  • Clarification of edge cases, exceptions, and non-standard data entries
  • Establishment of delivery formats (Excel, CSV, database injection, or portal entry)
Stage Deliverable: Scope Specification & Output Template Calibration
Aligned & Signed
02
Stage Two

Planning

Define workflow, responsibilities and timelines. We structure the engagement into clear operational milestones, assemble the team, assign a dedicated project lead, and formalize step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Key Operational Activities:
  • Creation of documented Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and decision trees
  • Resource allocation and assignment of dedicated team leads and operators
  • Establishment of realistic batch delivery schedules and turnaround SLAs
  • Secure access provisioning under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
Stage Deliverable: Approved SOP Checklist, Timeline Schedule & Team Roster
Workflow Ready
03
Stage Three

Execution

Perform the agreed data entry and processing tasks. Dedicated associates execute the work strictly following documented SOP checklists within designated software platforms, spreadsheets, or cloud applications.

Key Operational Activities:
  • High-precision manual and software-assisted data entry and alphanumeric extraction
  • Data processing: sorting, cleansing, deduplication, schema validation, and database updates
  • Systematic logging of anomalous records into a structured exception register
  • Team lead supervision to maintain steady hourly unit throughput and schedule adherence
Stage Deliverable: Processed Data Batches & Exception Register
Active Production
04
Stage Four

Quality Review

Check completed work against agreed requirements. An independent quality assurance (QA) auditor conducts comprehensive secondary reviews and statistical checks to verify data fidelity before release.

Key Operational Activities:
  • Dual-pass auditing: independent verification of entries against original source files
  • Algorithmic and rule-based validation (field formats, required headers, duplicate checks)
  • Root-cause defect analysis and immediate correction of identified discrepancies
  • Preparation of audit scorecards summarizing verification pass rates and sample sizes
Stage Deliverable: Validated Datasets & Quality Audit Scorecard
QA Verified
05
Stage Five

Delivery

Deliver work and collect feedback. We transmit verified deliverables through your preferred secure transfer channels, deliver completion ledgers, and hold regular calibration reviews to ensure continuous operational improvement.

Key Operational Activities:
  • Secure handover via encrypted transfer, target cloud repository, or portal commit
  • Comprehensive delivery report detailing record counts, batch status, and resolution logs
  • Structured client feedback collection to calibrate SOPs for upcoming cycles
  • Ongoing operational optimization to increase throughput and reduce cycle times
Stage Deliverable: Final Deliverable Package, Handover Ledger & Feedback Review
Completed & Calibrated
